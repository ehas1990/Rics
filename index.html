Our RICS Home Survey Services

Choose the Right RICS Home Survey

Different properties require different levels of inspection. We offer RICS Home Survey Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 options tailored to factors such as property age, size, condition, complexity and construction.

RICS Home Survey Level 1

A straightforward overview of your property's condition
Ideal for conventional, newer or well-maintained properties constructed using common building materials.
Our Level 1 survey provides a concise assessment of the property's condition and highlights significant defects, risks and issues that may require attention.

Learn About Level 1

RICS Home Survey Level 2

A detailed survey for conventional properties
Our Level 2 Home Survey provides a detailed visual inspection for conventional houses, flats and bungalows in reasonable condition.
It highlights visible defects, repairs and maintenance needs. Level 2 is available as survey only or survey and valuation, where applicable.

Learn About Level 2

RICS Home Survey Level 3

A comprehensive survey for older, larger or complex properties
The Level 3 Home Survey is the most detailed RICS option, suitable for older, larger, unusual, altered or run-down properties, or where major works are planned.
It provides an in-depth assessment of condition, defects, repairs, maintenance requirements and repair costs clearly.

Learn About Level 3

An RICS Home Survey is a professional inspection and report that provides information about the condition of a residential property. The survey level determines the extent and detail of the inspection.

It depends on the property's age, size, condition, construction and complexity. Level 1 is designed for a basic overview, Level 2 provides a more detailed inspection for conventional properties, and Level 3 provides the most comprehensive assessment.

For many buyers, a survey provides valuable information about the property's condition before completing a purchase. It can highlight defects and potential problems that may influence your decision or require further investigation.

Level 2 is generally suited to conventional properties in reasonable condition, while Level 3 provides a more detailed assessment and is particularly suitable for older, larger, unusual, altered or run-down properties.

No. An RICS Home Survey Level 3 focuses on the condition of the property and does not include a market valuation.

There is generally no legal requirement for a buyer to obtain a survey in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, but RICS strongly recommends getting a home survey before purchasing.
