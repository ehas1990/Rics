RICS Level 3 Home Survey

RICS Level 3

A comprehensive assessment of your property

Comprehensive
Building Survey

The RICS Home Survey – Level 3, also known as a Building Survey, provides a thorough inspection and detailed assessment of a property's condition. It is particularly suitable for older, unusual, altered or extended properties, or where major works are planned.

The survey considers the property's construction, accessible areas, defects, likely causes, potential consequences, repairs and maintenance requirements.

Our Survey

What the Survey Covers

Services & Grounds

Visible services, permanent outbuildings and grounds are considered during the inspection, although services are inspected visually and not tested.

Thorough Property Inspection

A more detailed inspection of the main building, permanent outbuildings and grounds within the property boundary.

Repairs & Maintenance

Practical information about repairs, maintenance and the condition of the property to help inform your decision.

Defects & Their Causes

Detailed descriptions of defects, their most probable causes and the likely consequences if they are not addressed.

Construction & Materials

Consideration of the property's construction methods and materials as part of the detailed assessment.

Roof Space & Accessible Areas

Inspection includes accessible areas using ladders up to 3 metres and the roof space where accessible.

Condition Ratings

Condition Rating 1

No repair is currently needed. The property must be maintained in the normal way.

Condition Rating 2

Defects that need repairing or replacing but are not considered to be either serious or urgent. The property must be maintained in the normal way.

Condition Rating 3

Defects that are serious and/or need to be repaired, replaced or investigated urgently. Failure to do so could risk serious safety issues or severe long-term damage to your property. Written quotations for repairs should be obtained prior to legal commitment to purchase.

Not Inspected

The element was not inspected and the reason is given in the report. This may be because access was restricted or the element was not present at the property.

The inspection is visual and non-invasive. Carpets, floor coverings, furniture and secured panels are not moved or removed, and services are not specialist-tested.
